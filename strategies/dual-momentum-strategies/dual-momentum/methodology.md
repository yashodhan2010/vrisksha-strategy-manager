# Bombay Canopy Methodology

## Summary

Bamboo Canopy is our flagship rules-based momentum model portfolio that seeks to extract stronger price momentum signals while applying portfolio-level risk and diversification controls.

## Universe

Nifty 500

## Strategy Design

Top 35 equal weight stocks ranked using a proprietary model combining price trend, price momentum, and risk controls. 

## Portfolio Construction

The model portfolio includes a diversified basket of 35 equal weight holdings, subject to model constraints such as position sizing, sector exposure, and cash allocation rules. It rebalances bi-weekly to optimally harvest price momentum whie balancing turnover. If no stocks qualify for ranking, the portfolio sits in cash. Irregular rebalances may be triggered during extreme events.

## Rebalance

Bi-weekly

## Cash Allocation Rules

When the model has fewer qualifying opportunities or portfolio constraints prevent full equity deployment, the residual allocation may remain in cash or a cash-equivalent proxy.
